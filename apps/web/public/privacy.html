Deadminton privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version

Deadminton does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (volumes, controls, accessibility options), your challenge progress and the replay of your last match in your device's local storage, so they are there next time you play. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes it.

Replays you save

SAVE REPLAY downloads a small file with the match settings and the button presses of the match. It contains no personal information, and it only goes where you choose to send it.

The website

The web version is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. Deadminton itself does not use them.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
